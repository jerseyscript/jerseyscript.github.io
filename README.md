## Talk at our Meetup

Beginning in April 2026, we have featured speakers lead table talks at our meetup. You get 15 minutes to present a topic to a handful of peers, then we open it up to discussion. It's a great place to talk about the current state of software developement, share bold ideas, or workshop a topic that you plan to bring to a larger venue in the future. We ask that any resources that you share during your talk are also posted in the #jerseyscript channel in the [BoroJS Slack](https://join.slack.com/t/borojs/shared_invite/zt-3mik7cshz-E2dodUsYe9oqNrryE~UTKQ).

Submit your proposal for a talk by [opening an issue](https://github.com/jerseyscript/jerseyscript.github.io/issues) in this repository, and selecting the JerseyScript Talk Submission template.
